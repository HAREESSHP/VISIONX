Who We Are

About VisionX

VisionX is a dynamic, student-driven club at Guru Nanak Institutions committed to creating impactful and memorable campus experiences. The club serves as a platform where students from diverse academic backgrounds come together to explore creativity, innovation, leadership, and collaboration.

At its core, VisionX focuses on organizing engaging events, competitions, and showcases that encourage active participation and foster a vibrant campus culture. From technology-driven competitions and gaming tournaments to automobile showcases and cultural performances, VisionX creates opportunities that allow students to explore their interests beyond the classroom.

Auto Expo

Auto Expo Showcase

The Auto Expo is a spectacular pre-event that brings together automobile enthusiasts, innovators, and students to showcase the latest in automotive technology, design, and creativity. From classic cars to futuristic concepts, the Auto Expo is a celebration of engineering marvels and passion for automobiles. Experience live demonstrations, interactive exhibits, and a vibrant display of vehicles that inspire and excite the campus community.

Summit

Summit Guests

We are organizing this summit to bring successful industry leaders, innovators, and experienced professionals to our campus so that students can interact with them directly and gain practical exposure beyond classroom learning. By inviting 10+ distinguished guests, we aim to inspire students, help them understand real-world opportunities, guide them toward their career paths, and keep them on track with current industry trends, skills, and future possibilities. The summit creates a platform where students can learn from the journeys, experiences, and insights of accomplished people.
